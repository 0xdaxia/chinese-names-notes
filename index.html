Chinese Names Resource Notes

I'm Yechen, an indie developer who builds Mingzi, a free tool that helps people outside China find an authentic Chinese name with real meaning, correct pinyin and audio pronunciation. This page collects the notes I keep sharing with learners, writers and parents.

Turning an English name into Chinese

A phonetic transliteration is the quickest route, but it often sounds odd to native speakers. If you want something that sounds natural, try the English-to-Chinese name converter, which suggests both sound-alike and meaning-based options.

Generating a name from scratch

If you'd rather start fresh, the Chinese name generator on Mingzi lets you pick a surname, gender and the qualities you want the characters to carry.
